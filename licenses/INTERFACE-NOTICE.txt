AniCanto interface icons

Several shared stroke-icon geometries in studio/web/icons.js match or adapt
Feather's icon set. Preserve licenses/common/Feather-MIT.txt when distributing
the interface. This acknowledgement conservatively covers the corresponding
geometry; it does not claim that every AniCanto icon is an upstream icon.

Source: https://github.com/feathericons/feather/tree/v4.29.2

and names for AniCanto's controls. No endorsement is implied.
