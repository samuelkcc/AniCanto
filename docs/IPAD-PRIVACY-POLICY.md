## English

This policy applies to AniCanto for iPad, provided by SCKC. It does not describe the desktop edition or GitHub's own website data processing.

### On-device processing

Video recognition, translation, dubbing and subtitle processing take place on your iPad. AniCanto does not upload your videos, recordings, subtitles, scripts or generated content to the developer or third-party AI servers.

### Network connections

Downloading or repairing models connects to model file providers, which receive connection information needed to deliver the download, such as your IP address. These downloads do not include your videos, audio or scripts. Apple handles system resources and App Store purchases and restoration.

### Files, sharing and backups

Imported copies, job progress, recordings and outputs are stored in the app. Content is handed to your chosen app or file provider, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive, only when you choose to share or save a copy. iPadOS backups and cloud services follow your system and account settings.

### Logs and deletion

AniCanto includes no advertising-tracking or usage-analytics SDK and does not automatically send usage logs to the developer. Process logs stay on your device; you decide whether to export or share them. Delete corresponding content from the video list, output library or Model management. Copies saved elsewhere must be deleted separately at that location.

### Contact and updates

For a general privacy question, contact SCKC through GitHub Issues listed on the [support page](../SUPPORT.md#contact-sckc). Issues are public: describe your concern without posting personal data, private videos, recordings, scripts or purchase receipts. Support content you choose to post is hosted by GitHub and handled under the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) and its account/content settings, separately from files stored locally in the app. This policy will be updated if our data-handling practices change.

Effective date: 23 October 2026
